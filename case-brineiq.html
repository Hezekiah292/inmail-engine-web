CASE STUDY · INVESTOR OUTREACH

BrineIQ — investor-mandate calls booked in energy, within days of first touch

$20M–$500M+ energy transactions — venture partners replying, accepting, and booking themselves in.

Background

BrineIQ advises on $20M–$500M+ energy transactions — structuring institutional capital stacks and matching them with mandates from family offices and funds. Their challenge: getting live deals in front of the right investment partners without months of cold prospecting.

The warm-intro system we ran

We built a list of venture partners and fund principals actively deploying in energy — clean energy, oil & gas, transition assets — mapped the warmest path to each, and opened with one short, specific message about deals sitting directly in their strike box.

No pitch decks attached, no pressure — just relevance. A full-time LinkedIn rep handled every reply and moved interested partners straight to a Calendly.

The result

Partners accepted, replied, and booked mandate calls themselves — within 48 hours of first touch. Below: two real threads, start to finish.

THREAD 1 · TIM — VENTURE PARTNER
"Thanks, Danny. I have set up a call for Monday."
THREAD 2 · MAX — FUND PRINCIPAL
"I just scheduled time for tomorrow at 1pm MDT."

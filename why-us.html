WHY LINKEDIN

Why not the alternatives — and why LinkedIn

Cold outreach is dead — it's just outreach with no trust behind it. Every alternative fails for the same reason.

Not cold email
Anyone can spin up a fake domain, so investors and buyers just check your LinkedIn anyway — and on email all you can do is pitch, not build a relationship. Worse: it's one shot. If they don't respond, you can't contact that person again without becoming spam.
Not cold calls
Call an angel — or a VP with a budget — at 10am and it's "where did you even get my number?" You've burned the number and the first impression in one call, with no second way in.
Not connectors
Fund managers hand you the next investor they happen to know — not the one who's actually deployed in your industry. Their best investor? Already introduced to someone else. You get leftovers.
Not automation & outbound tools or agencies
Blast tools and volume agencies tarnish your reputation by spamming your market in your name. Once your dream investors, customers, and companies flag you, that market is poisoned — and you only had one shot at each of them.
Warm introductions: 20 ways in, not one shot
Cold outreach gives you exactly one attempt per person. With warm paths there are up to 20 ways to reach the same investor, customer, or company — through the different people they already trust. And LinkedIn is where they all live: C-level, angels, syndicates, family offices. Because LinkedIn caps everyone (25 connections/week, InMails $3–5), they're barely contacted there. The limit is the moat.

THE PROBLEM

Why most companies fail with outreach

After running outreach for hundreds of founders and B2B teams, it's almost always the same three reasons.

1
Wrong targets
Investors who don't fund their stage or industry. Customers who were never going to buy. Companies with no reason to partner. Months and thousands of dollars burned on the wrong rooms.
2
Cold, not warm
90% of capital raised — and deals closed — comes through warm introductions, not cold outreach. Your network already has paths to the people you need; you just can't see them.
3
Not enough volume
The 17x rule — the US deal flow & venture rule: you need pipeline worth ~17× your target, whether that's a raise or a revenue goal. Hundreds of real opportunities — with no way to create them at scale.

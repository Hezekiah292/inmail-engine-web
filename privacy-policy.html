Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 21, 2026

InMail Engine ("we," "us," or "our") operates inmailengine.com and provides LinkedIn outreach and investor-introduction services (the "Services"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using our website or Services, you agree to this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide: name, email address, company details, phone number, and any information you submit through forms, calls, or when engaging our Services — including LinkedIn and email account data you authorize us to access to map warm paths and run campaigns on your behalf.

Information collected automatically: IP address, browser type, device information, pages visited, and usage data collected via cookies and similar technologies.

Information from third parties: business and professional data from publicly available sources, data providers, and platforms such as LinkedIn, used to deliver the Services.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to provide and improve the Services; build investor lists and map warm introduction paths; run and optimize outreach campaigns you engage us for; communicate with you about your account, campaigns, and offers; process payments; comply with legal obligations; and protect against fraud and misuse.

3. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with: service providers who help us operate (hosting, analytics, payment processing, data enrichment), under confidentiality obligations; parties you direct us to contact as part of your campaigns; professional advisors; and authorities when required by law. 4. Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies to operate the site, remember preferences, and analyze traffic. You can control cookies through your browser settings; disabling them may affect site functionality.

5. Data Retention & Security

We retain personal information only as long as needed for the purposes described above or as required by law, then delete or anonymize it. 6. Your Rights & Choices

Depending on your state of residence (including California under the CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal information, and to opt out of certain sharing. We do not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

To exercise any right, or to opt out of marketing emails (also available via the unsubscribe link in any message), contact us at info@inmailengine.com.

Our Services are for businesses and are not directed to individuals under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Contact Us

Questions about this policy or our data practices: info@inmailengine.com.
